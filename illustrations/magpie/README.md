# Magpie character studies

Three raster illustration concepts derived from the bird inside `public/wordmark.svg` and `public/mark.svg`. The head's circular eye, open beak, rounded silhouette and blue/white identity inform the new full-body character. The body and poses are new interpretations, not existing official brand assets.

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Original logo files and homepage composition remain unchanged. PNG files have transparent backgrounds.

- `magpie-standing.png`: standing profile.
- `magpie-flying.png`: wings raised in flight.
- `magpie-looking-back.png`: looking over its shoulder.

## Generation prompts

### Image 1

Create a standalone Magpie bird character illustration derived faithfully from the bird embedded in the attached Apache Magpie wordmark and M symbol. References are brand identity references, not layouts to reproduce. Use case: illustration-story. Extend the existing head design into a full-body bird, maintaining the large circular white eye ring with blue pupil, rounded blue head, distinct slightly open triangular two-part beak pointing right, smooth white cheek/throat cutout. The bird is a magpie, with a long tapered tail and a compact body, NOT a parrot or owl. Pose: calm standing side profile facing right, curious alert posture, two simple feet, folded wing. Flat bold cobalt blue matching the logo, white negative-space wing and breast, clean rounded contours, restrained geometric shapes. Sophisticated graphic editorial mascot suitable for a developer community website, not a baby cartoon. Single bird centered, ample margin, square composition, true transparent background. No letters, no M surrounding container, no wordmark, no text, no scenery, no gradients, no shadow, no 3D. The original face identity is the priority.
