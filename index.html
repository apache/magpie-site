Who’s using Magpie

Airflow maintainers use Magpie to investigate security reports and prepare fixes.

“Using Magpie helped us to do things that matter”
Jarek PotiukApache Airflow® PMC member

What does Magpie do here?

Magpie’s skills cover the whole process, from checking a security report to fixing the problem, releasing the fix, and closing the report.
