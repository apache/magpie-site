Magpie at Apache Airflow

As contributions grew, the Airflow maintainers put Magpie to work on the time-consuming parts of managing issues, pull requests, and security reports.

Jarek PotiukApache Magpie PMC chair · Apache Airflow PMC member

The maintainer’s perspective

“In February 2026 things changed for Airflow for good. We started to get more and more PRs, and Security Issues and since then, it only accelerated. Without Magpie, that allowed us to handle 10x more issues and PRs, with 3x the speed that we used to have, we would be likely to throw our towels and give up”.

“Using Magpie helped us to do things that matter and let our agents do all the stuff that made us - at the beginning of the AI-generated growth of contributions - go down the spiral of burn-out and desperation.”

Testimonial supplied by Jarek Potiuk. The 10× and 3× figures describe his assessment of the team’s experience; they are not a controlled benchmark.

Airflow’s public user list includes Airbnb, Adobe, and Spotify. These are users of Airflow, not claimed Magpie adopters.

These are observations from a single project. The all-time totals do not isolate the effect of Magpie or establish a before-and-after comparison.
